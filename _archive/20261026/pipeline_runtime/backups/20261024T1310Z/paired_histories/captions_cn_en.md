# Paired processing-history illustration / 同母帧处理示意

The five rows cover all H source method labels and were fixed by lexicographic (video_uid, mother_frame_index, mother_id) selection within frozen common TRAIN, before reading any detector scores. Columns retain the full saved mother, direct B crop, A intermediate, AB crop after A redetection, and AB validity mask (white valid, black padding). Image aspect ratios are retained; saved pixels are displayed with nearest interpolation, without new face detection, alignment, cropping or retouching. Padding fractions are recomputed from the saved masks. A/B/AB byte, RGB, mask and parent-chain identities are verified. Examples may share source people or ancestry and are illustrative, not independent evidence of image quality, manipulated-face identity, or model accuracy. No target TEST performance or H GPU qualification is claimed.
